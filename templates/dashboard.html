How is this calculated?

Each skill's proficiency is estimated from every attempt on that topic (interview answers and DSA submissions), not a single answer. Recent attempts count more, and with little evidence the score is pulled towards a neutral 50%, so one bad answer can't sink a skill. A skill is rated only after 3 attempts, and confidence grows as you answer more.
